Overview

What is Personal OS?

Personal OS is a self-management dashboard designed to bring the moving pieces of daily life into one organized space. Instead of separating tasks, projects, journals, meals, routines, and planning into different tools, the system is being built around connected context.

The current local version focuses on helping a user understand what is scheduled, what is overdue, what has gone stale, what needs attention, and what area of life or work should become the next focus.

Personal OS is not a public product yet. This page documents the active local build, the product direction, the systems already taking shape, and the decisions I am making as the project evolves.

The goal is to show progress honestly while giving visitors a clear view into how I think through product architecture, user experience, and feature development over time.

Problem

The Problem It Is Being Built Around

Productivity tools often focus on isolated pieces: a task list, a calendar, a notes app, or a habit tracker. The challenge is that real life does not always separate itself that neatly.

Personal OS is being built around the idea that tasks, projects, meals, routines, journals, and plans are connected. A useful system should help make those connections visible instead of forcing the user to mentally track everything alone.
