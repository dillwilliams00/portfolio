My Process

How I Approach a Build

I use each project as both a product and a learning opportunity. The goal is not only to ship something useful, but to understand the decisions behind the build.

01

Define

I clarify the problem, target users, and reason the product needs to exist.

02

Design

I think through user experience, page structure, flows, accessibility, and visual direction.

03

Build

I develop in stages, starting with structure and layout before moving deeper into logic and data.

04

Improve

I document what worked, what broke, what I learned, and what needs to improve next.
